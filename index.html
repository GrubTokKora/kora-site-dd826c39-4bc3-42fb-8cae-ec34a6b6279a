Welcome to Kumo — Sushi & Lounge

Kumo Sushi & Lounge is an inspiration derived from traditional Japanese culture. Our comfortable atmosphere and the high quality cuisine have made it possible to open our third Kumo in Scarsdale. Our highly trained chefs always select the freshest ingredients to prepare in each meal according to individual tastes. Kumo in Scarsdale is designed to provide visual creativity in both preparation and presentation. Here at Kumo we strive to give each of our customers an incredibly satisfying dining experience.
