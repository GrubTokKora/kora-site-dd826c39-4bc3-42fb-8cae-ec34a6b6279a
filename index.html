Our Story

Where Japanese
Craft Meets Lounge

KUMO Sushi Lounge is an inspiration derived from traditional Japanese culture — a sleek, intimate izakaya in the heart of Scarsdale offering sushi, hibachi, and an array of traditional Japanese fare in an atmosphere that invites you to linger.

Plush seating, considered lighting, and a curated drinks programme create a lounge experience that is at once modern and deeply rooted in the Japanese tradition of gathering, sharing, and savouring.
